# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a professional, inclusive, and harassment-free community.
Participation in this repository must remain respectful, constructive, and
focused on improving the Reopt Data SDK integration experience.

## Expected behavior

- Give specific, actionable, and technically grounded feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of a contribution directly.
- Respect privacy, consent, and security boundaries in examples and reports.
- Keep disagreements about ideas; do not attack or demean people.
- Credit the work and ideas of other contributors accurately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of
private information, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, and retaliation
against good-faith reporters are not tolerated.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue. Contact the repository
maintainers through a private channel listed on the
[`reopt-ai` organization profile](https://github.com/reopt-ai). Include the
repository name and enough context for a fair review.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or take
other proportionate action. Reports will be handled as privately as practical,
with conflicts of interest disclosed and avoided.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is representing this
project in public or private project-related communication.
